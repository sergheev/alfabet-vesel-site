Privacy Policy — Play & Learn

In short: the app collects no information about you or your child.

What data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it sends nothing over the internet by itself.

What stays on the device

The games the child has finished, the rewards, the sound settings and the language choice are stored only on the phone or tablet. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it.

Purchases

The remaining games unlock with a single in-app purchase through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we do not see or keep any card or payment details.

Children

The app is made for children aged 2 to 5 and asks them for no information. Settings and the purchase are behind a parental gate.

Voice

The spoken words are recorded voice clips that ship inside the app (and, only if a clip were missing, the speech voice built into the device). The app does not record audio and never uses the microphone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address.
